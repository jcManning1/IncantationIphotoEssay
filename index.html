Installation portfolio

About

This is a portfolio for one work. The entrance is a poster. The inner room is a photo essay of ten plates, read in order.

Enter GET OUT OF YOUR HEAD and the soundscape starts on its own. It plays from the beginning, loops when it ends, and stops only when you leave the room.
